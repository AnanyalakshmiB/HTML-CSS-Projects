BEFORE YOU GO

Simple Travel Tips

01

Plan Your Route

Keep your destinations close together to make the most of your trip.

02

Carry Essentials

Carry comfortable shoes, water, sunscreen and a light jacket for hill destinations.

03

Respect Nature

Keep tourist places clean and avoid disturbing wildlife.
